## Closed comparison and experimentation gaps

Official history now answers how far the latest result is ahead or behind the chosen reference, which sector is the largest opportunity, which sector improved, how incidents and Flow changed, and whether plan or conditions confound attribution. Players may pin a retained official result as a durable reference; otherwise PB remains the default. Every sector is browsable with exact times and a signed delta. Guidance selects technique, matched repetition, or a one-variable experiment accordingly. Garage and browser-readable state consume the same analysis; applying a recorded plan remains transactional and preserves live wear and odometer state.

Saved builds now expose quantified current-versus-saved and occupied pairwise runtime consequences for drive, grip, speed, suspension, and braking across eight durable slots. Comparison is explicitly read-only, so the choice can be inspected without silently changing the active bike.

Opponent difficulty now exposes five ordered profiles rather than three coarse steps. Every positive late-pressure, close-attack, and Flow contribution shares one inspectable cap; deterministic telemetry reports raw/applied assistance, clamp counts, contact and crash rates, and the 2.5-second launch-safety window. Nearby rival Flow attacks identify the rider and signature trait through HUD, warning audio, and captions. Opponent contact geometry is explicit and visual-sized while deterministic manual resolution remains symmetric.

## Release rule

The product may be called technically release-ready after the automated and packaged-browser checks pass. It may be called validated for fun, learning, accessibility, feel, or real-device performance only after the matching external gates pass with recorded evidence.
