## Current release evidence

- Full source gate: parser/editor scan, all 99 focused probes, and Academy, Circuit, Pine Enduro, Mesa MX, Freestyle, and Discovery smokes pass.
- Opponent challenge: the deterministic 16-simulation matrix covers all three tracks and difficulty tiers; strong and elite pressured samples each execute 51 tactical Flow boosts while preserving starter/upgraded placement and replay-identity contracts.
- Route authority: Quarry, Pine, Mesa, and reverse checks retain exactly one streamed track child. The gate caught an early sponsor-presenter ownership regression; moving that presenter outside `LevelRoot` restored `active_children=1`.
- Academy integrity: all ten lessons retain normalized HUD scopes, device-correct coaching, deterministic grading, rematches, rewards, and persistence. Surface Reading and Grip uses the real bike’s hardpack, sand, grass, and mud response.
- Sponsor integrity: Dustline Works, Wildbrush Outpost, and Sundown Static advance independently through four ranks with exact objectives and rewards. Ordinary routes receive one collision-free downcourse arch plus a finish board; Freestyle/Discovery use bounded open-area boards; Academy/Test Ride remain commercial-free.
- Track evolution integrity: real player/opponent passages form five-lane, 12-meter route-bin wear with rider/bin de-duplication, bounded dry benefit and wet risk, one batched zero-collision visual, restart cleanup, and Academy/Test Ride isolation. Circuit, Pine, and Mesa smokes each form `33–40` visible grooves.
- Variable-weather integrity: Mesa Six-Lap exposes the complete dry/wind/cloud/rain/storm/drying forecast before the race. The same deterministic per-lap condition drives bike handling and wear, opponent wet-surface skill, roost/audio, evolving-rut policy, gradual atmosphere, live HUD/current-next warnings, and browser state.
- Crash accessibility integrity: the Access page exposes Standard/Assisted crash support. Assisted halves lasting landing/recovery damage and recognizes a tipped bike in `0.6555` seconds instead of `1.15`, while reset counts and time penalties remain unchanged. The setting uses schema 16 verified persistence, freezes per activity, appears in schema-7 browser state, and racecraft revision 8 separates Standard and Assisted records.
